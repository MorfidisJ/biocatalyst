Key Questions

Do I need established project ideas? ★
Not at all. You can join the session completely open-minded, link up with an active workspace collective during team formations, and launch right into development.
What criteria will judges utilize? ★
Real utility, raw prototype functionality, architectural execution, and how creative your solution addresses complex bottlenecks. No fluff slides allowed.
